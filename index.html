Part 02 · 7 minutes

WBG mission, priorities & Uganda

Answer from what you know, then use the source links in the debrief to close gaps.

Knowledge · context · relevance
Current context to know: On 4 June 2026, the WBG endorsed a ten-year Uganda Country Partnership Framework (2026–2035). Its central pathway out of poverty is creating more and better jobs, supported by stronger economic governance, healthier and better-skilled people, better-connected communities and a more productive, inclusive private sector. The WBG reports that 600,000–700,000 young people enter Uganda's labour market each year. See official source links in the debrief.

Part 03 · 12 minutes

Technical judgment: IFC E&S

The role requires appraisal support, documentation review, gap analysis, ESAP tracking, monitoring and clear communication to investment teams.

Due diligence · supervision
Case file · fictional investment

GreenLink Logistics Corridor

An IFC investment team is considering financing a logistics and warehousing company expanding near a wetland and several farming communities. The client has supplied an ESIA from four years ago, a draft ESMS, incomplete contractor safety records, and an Annual Monitoring Report that lists three overdue actions as “in progress.” A community group has recently reported blocked access to fields and a small fuel spill near a drainage channel. The investment team asks for a concise E&S view before its internal meeting tomorrow.

Your task: choose the best immediate approach, then write a short recommendation that is candid about evidence gaps and next actions.

Technical knowledge · 4 items1 point each

Choose the single best answer.

Case response · 6 minutesAnalytical writing

Write a concise E&S briefing for the investment team. What would you say now, and what must happen next?

Address material risks, what is known versus unknown, immediate protective steps, missing evidence, proposed action owners/deadlines, and what can or cannot be concluded before the meeting. Do not pretend that the old ESIA or a label of “in progress” proves current compliance.

Part 04 · 8 minutes

Values, collaboration & delivery

Use real examples where possible. If you have not encountered a situation, explain your intended approach and escalation logic.

Urgency · risk · accountability
Question 1 · 4 minutesMulticultural teamwork

Describe a time you worked with people whose professional, cultural or personal perspectives differed from yours. How did you make the collaboration effective, and what changed because of your contribution?

Include how you listened, handled disagreement, made space for less-heard voices, and delivered a shared result. Avoid claiming that “good communication” alone solved the problem.

Suggested length: 150–200 words0 words
Question 2 · 4 minutesThree culture attributes together

An investment officer asks for your E&S recommendation by close of business. You find a potentially serious issue, but a key report is missing. Your manager is in meetings. What do you do?

Demonstrate sense of urgency, thoughtful risk-taking, empowerment and accountability. Speed means prioritising and communicating; it never means hiding a material uncertainty.

Part 05 · 5 minutes

Learning agility & future potential

A strong YP signals curiosity through action: asks precise questions, seeks feedback, tests understanding, and applies learning.

Growth · humility · initiative
Question 1 · 3 minutesLearning from feedback

Tell us about a time you received feedback that changed how you worked, or had to learn an unfamiliar technical area quickly. How did you check that your learning improved the result?

Name the feedback or knowledge gap, the steps you took, how you sought help, how you applied it, and the evidence that changed.

Suggested length: 130–180 words0 words
Question 2 · 2 minutesQuestions you would ask

If selected, what are two precise questions you would ask your manager or mentor in your first month to learn faster and contribute responsibly?

Ask questions that reveal how work is judged, how risk decisions are made, how the team learns from supervision, or where your contribution could reduce friction. Avoid questions answered by a quick website search.
